# Hydroid Mobile App — Veids.in Theme

A mobile application interface designed according to the official **Veids Ventures** (`veids.in`) brand design language and aesthetics.

## Design System & Theme Alignment

- **Brand Typography**: Google Font **DM Sans** (`wght@400..1000`) with tight letter-spacing (`-0.04em` to `-0.05em`) and uppercase tracking eyebrows (`letter-spacing: 0.16em`).
- **Color Palette**:
  - **Veids Primary Green**: `#205c4b`
  - **Deep Pine / Headlines**: `#143e34`, `#113e31`
  - **Pastel Mint & Sage Gradients**: `radial-gradient(ellipse at 70% 50%, #eef4dc 0%, #c6dbc3 42%, #92b899 100%)`
  - **Veids Paper Canvas**: `#fbfcf9`
  - **ActiveSense Cyan LED Light**: `#00e5ff` / `#22d3ee`
- **Branding**: Official **VEIDS VENTURES PVT. LTD.** logo with multi-toned water droplet mark.

## Key Views & Features

1. **Home Tab**:
   - Faithful recreation of the `veids.in` hero banner: *"Hydration. A little smarter."*
   - White pill button *"Join The Revolution ↗"* and circular down-arrow scroll cue.
   - Core philosophy card: *"The future of water starts with knowing what's inside"*.
   - Live paired bottle status (`Hydroid Pro Connected`, 85% battery, `UV-C Clean`).
   - Smart water metrics (TDS Level at 148 ppm, Crisp 19°C temperature).
   - Bottle controls (UV-C Sterilization, ActiveSense Water Scan, Veids AI Coach, Store).
   - Water remaining progress bar (850ml / 2000ml) & active mineral cartridge pods (Magnesium, Calcium, Zinc).
   - 12-day streak & Hydrator Pro milestone level.
2. **History Tab**:
   - Interactive SVG analytics graph with toggles for **TDS Purity**, **Water Intake**, and **Minerals Dosed**.
   - Timeframe filter (Weekly, Monthly, Yearly).
   - Scan records log with timestamps, source, dosage, and 99.9% clean certification.
3. **Discover / Store Tab**:
   - Showcase of the Hydroid Smart Bottle with interactive edition toggle (**Matte Navy Steel** vs. **Pure Crystal Glass**).
   - Official pricing and pre-order pill button.
   - ActiveSense 3-pack mineral refill cartridges (Daily Essential, Performance Electrolytes).
4. **Profile Tab**:
   - User identity, paired BLE 5.3 bottle hardware diagnostics (firmware, battery health, sterilization count), and reminder interval settings.
5. **Interactive Center PUSH Button & Modals**:
   - Center navigation button modeled after the bottle cap with animated cyan LED ring.
   - **ActiveSense Pipeline Modal**: Live 5-step analysis sequence (*Scan → Analyze → Correct → Verify → Ready*) with glowing radar and telemetry readouts.
   - **Veids Hydroid Coach Modal**: AI hydration chat with suggested prompts and responsive conversational feedback.
   - **VIP Join The Revolution Modal**: Waitlist registration dialog.
